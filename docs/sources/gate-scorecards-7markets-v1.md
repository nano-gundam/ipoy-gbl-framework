## 1. Scope and geographic treatment

SE3 is the southern Swedish bidding zone, not the whole Swedish national electricity market. This scorecard applies the following geographic hierarchy:

- **National Swedish evidence:** legal and regulatory framework, BRP/BSP access, PPA market, GoO/EECS and national policy.
- **SE3-specific evidence:** installed generation capacity, zone-level futures and EPAD evidence, and any available zone-specific connection data.
- **National proxy for SE3:** Swedish national industrial/commercial demand and national corporate PPA / GoO statistics where no comparable SE3 series is available. These are explicitly labelled as proxies and must not be presented as SE3 totals.

SE3 should be described as a **nuclear-dominant southern Swedish bidding zone with significant hydropower inflows and growing wind generation**. It should not be described as hydro-dominant based on Sweden-wide generation statistics.

## 7. Priority follow-up and evidence gaps

### Before a country PASS can be considered

1. **G1-01 — BRP access:** Obtain written PSE and Polish BRP/retailer confirmation of direct foreign-entity eligibility, or a complete third-party route including licence/registration scope, collateral, onboarding timing, settlement and responsibilities.
2. **G3-01 — demand:** Extract the exact Poland values for Eurostat `nrg_bal_c`, SIEC E7000 Electricity, `FC_IND_E + FC_OTH_CP_E`, preferably 2023 and latest available 2024. Convert from TJ to TWh using 3,600 TJ per TWh. Do not use total national electricity consumption as a substitute.
3. **G5-02 — BESS:** Build a project-level list separating commissioned, under-construction and permitted/approved standalone BESS, with MW/MWh, location, date, source and pumped-hydro exclusion.
4. **G5-04 — MARI:** Re-check PSE’s actual operational accession after the planned 2 December 2026 date. Do not pre-score future integration as active.

### Additional commercial and legal diligence

- Obtain transaction-level evidence for Polish corporate PPAs, distinguishing corporate offtakers from utility/trader PPAs and recording MW, tenor, physical/financial form and counterparties.
- Obtain TGE forward-market volume, open interest and bid-ask spreads by product and tenor; do not infer liquidity from total RTPE turnover alone.
- Confirm current AIB/EECS electricity status and actual cross-border GO transfer route after the AIB 2025 report.
- For shortlisted assets, check node-level grid availability, flexible connection terms, redispatch and curtailment rights, and current DSO/TSO tariffs.
- Confirm the precise negative-price and certificate-of-origin effects for each renewable support regime and the intended contract structure.

## 8. Methodology and source limitations

- This is **Proposed v1**, pending Methodology & Validation sign-off. The 0/1/2/ND scale, five Mandatory checks, candidate KO rules and exact 16 checklist IDs are unchanged from PW03-MR-00.
- No Gate 2 economics indicator (baseline prices, volatility, negative-price share, capture rates or intraday spreads) is scored here.
- `ND` is used where the correct metric or route was not established. ND is not zero.
- The G3-01 demand metric is a national proxy, not a pure private-sector B2B TAM. The 20 TWh and 80 TWh bands remain working screening anchors pending Flower’s ticket-size input.
- National capacity snapshots are not asset- or node-level data. The supplied file contains no LT `Energy storage` rows, but it does contain a 900 MW pumped-hydro category. Missing BESS rows in that CSV are not proof of zero battery capacity.
- Litgrid’s October 2026 installed-capacity page is used to update the starting dataset: 1,199 MW reported battery technologies are separated from 900 MW pumped hydro. The 55 VERT permits are a project-development indicator, not a quantified construction/permitted MW pipeline.
- Baltic regulatory research is used only where it applies operationally to Lithuania; no Estonia or Latvia scorecard is created.
- Source facts and researcher interpretations are distinguished in each Evidence cell. Human review of underlying legislation, translations and live regulatory pages remains necessary before legal reliance.
- Research cut-off for this version: **16 November 2026**.
