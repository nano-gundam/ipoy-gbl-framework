# PW01-MR-04: Gate 3 Market-Size & Corporate PPA Base Dataset (20 Countries)

## 1. Traceability & Methodology Protocol (No False Precision)
To ensure 100% auditability and avoid fabricated precision:
- **Electricity Consumption Formula:** $\text{Total B2B Demand (TWh)} = \frac{\text{FC\_IND\_E (GWh)} + \text{FC\_OTH\_CP\_E (GWh)}}{1000}$
  - `FC_IND_E`: Final consumption - industry sector - energy use (Eurostat table `nrg_bal_c`, SIEC `E7000` Electricity).
  - `FC_OTH_CP_E`: Final consumption - other sectors - commercial and public services - energy use (Eurostat table `nrg_bal_c`, SIEC `E7000` Electricity).
  - *Rounding Note:* Because raw Eurostat figures in GWh are summed prior to rounding to two decimal places in TWh, the sum of the displayed rounded components may differ from the displayed total by ±0.01 TWh.
- **Non-EU Exceptions:**
  - **Norway (NO):** Extracted directly via Eurostat API (`nrg_bal_c`) under EEA reporting obligations; cross-verified against Statistics Norway (SSB Table `08307`).
  - **United Kingdom (UK):** Exited Eurostat in 2020. Official figures are sourced from the UK Department for Energy Security and Net Zero (DESNZ) *Digest of United Kingdom Energy Statistics (DUKES) Chapter 5, Tables 5.1 & 5.2* (Industrial + Commercial consumption).
- **Corporate PPA Figures (2023–2025) & Source Disagreement Notes:**
  - Public figures are compiled from the **RE-Source Platform Deal Tracker**, **WindEurope**, and public summaries of the **Pexapark European PPA Market Outlook**.
  - **Where Sources Disagree:** Public trackers (RE-Source) only capture publicly announced corporate PPAs and omit confidential bilateral contracts or smaller merchant-sleeved deals. Paid trackers (Pexapark, BloombergNEF) report 15–25% higher contracted capacity where unannounced deals are disclosed privately, whereas RE-Source counts multi-offtaker club deals as multiple transactions. Values below reflect verified public tracker ranges rather than false exact integers.
